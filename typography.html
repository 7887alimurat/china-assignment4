Where tea began

Tea has been grown in China for thousands of years. According to legend, Emperor Shennong discovered tea when leaves fell into his boiling water. Later, tea became part of daily life, poetry and ceremonies.

Main types of Chinese tea

Chinese tea is usually divided into green, black, white, yellow, oolong and dark tea. The type depends on how the leaves are processed after picking.
