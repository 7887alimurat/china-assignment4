Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to show or hide the answer.

Most visitors need a visa, although several countries have visa-free or transit arrangements for short stays. Always check the current rules on the official embassy website before booking your tickets.

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the most comfortable weather in most regions. Avoid the Golden Week holiday in early October and Chinese New Year if you dislike crowds.

Alipay and WeChat Pay are accepted almost everywhere. Foreign bank cards can be linked to both apps, but it is wise to carry some cash and a card as a backup.

Many Western services are blocked. Install a roaming eSIM or a VPN before you arrive, and download offline maps and a translation app.

High-speed trains are fast, comfortable and connect almost all major cities. Tickets can be booked through the official 12306 app or a trusted travel agency.

The Great Wall and the Forbidden City in Beijing, the Terracotta Army in Xi'an, the Bund in Shanghai and the karst landscape of Guilin are the classic highlights.
