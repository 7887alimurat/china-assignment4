Attractions in China

China has many famous attractions. Tourists can visit historical places, temples, mountains and modern cities.

Explore Attractions Photo Carousel

01

Great Wall of China

The Great Wall of China is one of the most famous attractions in the country. It was built over many centuries and today millions of tourists visit it.

02

Forbidden City

The Forbidden City is located in Beijing. It was the imperial palace of Chinese emperors for hundreds of years.
