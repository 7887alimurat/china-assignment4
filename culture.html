Culture of China

Chinese culture is one of the oldest and richest in the world. It includes traditions, festivals, art forms and philosophies that continue to shape everyday life.

Traditions and Values

Traditions and Values

Chinese culture is deeply influenced by Confucianism, Taoism and Buddhism. Respect for family, harmony and education are important values.

  • Respect for elders and family traditions
  • Calligraphy and traditional painting
  • Tea culture
  • Traditional Chinese medicine
  • Martial arts such as Kung Fu and Tai Chi
