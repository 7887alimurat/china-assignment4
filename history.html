History of China

China has one of the longest continuous histories in the world. Dynasties rose and fell over thousands of years, leaving monuments, traditions and inventions.

Ancient Beginnings

Ancient Beginnings

Chinese civilization developed along the Yellow River. Early dynasties such as the Shang and Zhou helped shape writing, philosophy and government.

  • Development of early Chinese writing
  • Bronze casting techniques
  • Confucian and Taoist philosophy
  • Formation of centralized states
  • Silk Road trade routes

Imperial China

  1. Qin Dynasty - unification of China
  2. Han Dynasty - expansion of the Silk Road
  3. Tang Dynasty - major cultural development
  4. Ming Dynasty - construction of the Forbidden City
  5. Qing Dynasty - the last imperial dynasty
